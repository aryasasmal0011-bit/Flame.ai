IGNITING DISCOVERY. PROTECTING THE FUTURE.

Flame.ai transforms complex NASA microgravity fire experiment data into accessible scientific knowledge. Researchers, students and curious minds can move from raw experiment records to clear explanations, comparisons and answers.

Understanding how fire behaves away from Earth is part of keeping future crews safe. Flame.ai exists to make that understanding easier to reach.

FIRE BEHAVES DIFFERENTLY IN SPACE.

On Earth, gravity makes hot gas rise. Take gravity-driven buoyancy and convection away, and a flame is free to behave in ways we rarely see at home.
